Add two Sanring family notification blocks for sanring-notify: `block/notification-bell` (bell with unread count, a panel with the list, mark all as read, and loading / empty / error-with-retry states; the app fetches and turns notifications into text) and `block/announcement-banner` (full-width bars above the shell for active banners, optional link and dismiss).
